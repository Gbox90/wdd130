Orlando Is Working to Reduce Food Waste

The City of Orlando provides food-waste education and information about composting and waste prevention.

Residents are encouraged to purchase only what they need, use leftovers, store food properly, and compost unavoidable scraps.

Across the United States

The Impact Goes Beyond Florida

Food Supply

National research estimates that a significant share of the U.S. food supply is lost or wasted before it is eaten.

ReFED ↗

Landfill Methane

Food decomposing in landfills generates methane, a powerful greenhouse gas.

U.S. EPA ↗

Household Waste

Household behaviors including over-purchasing, forgotten leftovers, and confusion about food date labels contribute to food waste.

NRDC Report ↗

What Does the Date Label Mean?

Research has identified inconsistent food date labels as a contributor to unnecessary food disposal.

Consumers may interpret quality-based dates as safety deadlines and throw away food that may still be usable.

Wade (2023) ↗

Waste Has Upstream Effects

Food waste should be understood as part of a larger waste-management and circular-economy problem.

Producing food requires natural resources long before it reaches a household or restaurant.

van Ewijk & Stegemann (2023) ↗
What Gets Wasted?

Throwing Away Food Also Wastes...

Money

Households lose the money spent on food that is never eaten.

Water

Agriculture and food production require large amounts of water.

Energy

Processing, refrigeration, transportation, and cooking all consume energy.

Land

Farmland is used to produce food even when that food is never eaten.

Packaging

Packaging and transportation materials are also wasted when food is discarded.

Landfill Space

Food waste contributes to the amount of material local communities must collect and dispose of.

What Can We Do?

A Simple Food-Waste Hierarchy

01

Prevent

Plan meals, purchase realistic portions, and use food already available at home.

02

Reuse

Turn leftovers into lunches, soups, wraps, bowls, or another dinner.

03

Donate

When appropriate and safe, usable surplus food can be redirected to food-rescue programs.

04

Compost

Compost unavoidable food scraps instead of sending them to a landfill.
